# 00-152-03: ≥3 discovery interviews with target ICP

Feature: F152 (sdplab-qnyr)
Parent bead: sdplab-qnyr.3

## Goal

Conduct ≥3 structured customer-discovery interviews with the target ICP to validate pricing hypotheses and willingness-to-pay before committing to a pilot.

## Beads

- sdplab-qnyr.3

## Target ICP

Boutique consulting / agency / fractional CTO with 10–50 engineers and ≥8 AI-assisted PRs per week.

## Scope Files

- `docs/strategy/commercial-discovery/<date>-<org>.md` — one document per interview

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] ≥3 discovery interviews documented in `docs/strategy/commercial-discovery/`
- [ ] Each interview document covers:
  - Review-burden quantification (hours/week on PR review; % with AI assistance)
  - Current AI-PR governance pain (what breaks, how often)
  - Existing tooling (CodeRabbit, SonarQube, manual process)
  - Willingness-to-pay range explored explicitly
  - Competitive alternatives considered
  - Blockers to adoption (procurement, compliance, trust)
- [ ] Interview synthesis in `docs/strategy/commercial-discovery/synthesis.md`:
  - Confirms or refutes pricing hypothesis from 00-152-01 / 00-152-02
  - Identifies top 3 objections
  - States go/no-go signal for starting the pilot
- [ ] At least 1 interview conducted before the Wedge B pilot gate triggers

## Out of Scope

- Signed pilots or LOIs (tracked separately)
- Feature development based on interview feedback (tracked in F151+)

## Source Material

- Memo council synthesis §7 (Wedge B pilot gate: committed pilot before parallel resource allocation)
- IIP-council synthesis §Risks #2 (no validated buyer demand)

# Discovery Frame

**Raw idea:** build a crypto trading bot for retail investors

## Problem Statement

Retail investors lack the time, expertise, and consistent discipline to effectively execute crypto trading strategies, leading to missed opportunities, emotional decision-making, and potential losses in a volatile market.

## Jobs to Be Done

- A retail investor wants to automate their trading strategy to execute trades 24/7 without manual intervention.
- A novice trader wants to follow proven strategies or signals without needing deep technical or market analysis expertise.
- An active trader wants to backtest and optimize strategies based on historical data to improve potential returns.
- An investor wants to manage risk by setting automatic stop-losses and take-profit orders to protect their capital.

**Appetite:** large

**Scope:** A platform for retail investors to configure, automate, and monitor crypto trading bots, including strategy selection, backtesting, and risk management tools.

# Discovery Frame

**Raw idea:** AI meal planner that learns family preferences

## Problem Statement

Families struggle to consistently plan meals that accommodate diverse preferences, dietary needs, and time constraints, leading to stress, wasted food, and unhealthy choices.

## Jobs to Be Done

- Parents need to efficiently create weekly meal plans that satisfy all family members' tastes and nutritional requirements.
- Families want to reduce decision fatigue and time spent on meal planning while ensuring meals are enjoyable and varied.
- Individuals with dietary restrictions need to seamlessly integrate their needs into family meals without extra effort.

**Appetite:** medium

**Scope:** An AI-powered meal planning assistant that learns from family feedback, adapts to preferences over time, and generates personalized weekly plans with recipes and shopping lists.

# Discovery Experiment Brief

## Format: landing_page

**Objective:** Determine whether target users find the proposition compelling enough to click through and request access despite the perceived integration and workflow overhead.

**Hypothesis:** if we present a clear value proposition for automated architecture understanding, C4 generation, and drift detection for external polyglot repositories, then at least 8% of target users will click the CTA and at least 3% will request access within 7 days

**Success metric:** ≥8% CTA click-through rate and ≥3% access-request conversion from qualified visitors within 7 days

**Time box:** 7 days

**Testing claim:** teams are willing to invest in a new tool specifically for architectural understanding and documentation

## Setup Steps

1. Create a one-page landing page with a specific headline, 3 core outcomes, example screenshots/mockups of architecture diagrams and drift alerts, and a single CTA to request access or book a demo.
2. Drive a small, targeted batch of traffic from LinkedIn posts, relevant engineering communities, and direct outreach to 50-100 architects, tech leads, and CTOs at software teams working with polyglot codebases.
3. Track unique visits, CTA clicks, and completed access requests; segment responses by role and company size to see whether interest is concentrated in teams likely to tolerate integration effort.

## Required Tools

- simple landing page builder
- analytics tool
- email or LinkedIn outreach

# Discovery Hypothesis

**Raw idea:** build a CLI tool that monitors competitor pricing in real time

## Test Card (Strategyzer)

**We believe** product managers at mid-sized SaaS companies need to monitor competitor pricing changes in real-time because delayed responses lead to revenue loss in fast-moving markets

**To verify this**, we will a 5-question interview with 10 product managers to assess their current monitoring gaps and willingness to adopt a new tool

**We'll measure** percentage of interviewees confirming pricing delays cause strategic harm

**We are right if** 8 out of 10 interviewees rank real-time competitor pricing as a top-3 pain point within 2 weeks

## Assumptions (RAT-Ranked)

| Rank | Assumption | Risk | Uncertainty | RAT Score |
|------|-----------|------|-------------|----------|
| 1 | teams will prioritize this tool over manual research methods | high | high | 9 |
| 2 | real-time alerts will lead to faster strategic decisions | medium | high | 6 |
| 3 | competitor pricing data is publicly accessible and can be collected reliably | medium | medium | 4 |
| 4 | mid-sized companies lack existing automated solutions for this job | low | medium | 2 |
| 5 | product managers have authority to adjust pricing based on competitor data | low | low | 1 |

**Riskiest assumption (rank 1):** teams will prioritize this tool over manual research methods

## Requirements

- user can set up automated tracking for specific competitor products
- user receives real-time alerts when competitor prices change
- user can view historical pricing trends in a simple dashboard

# Discovery Hypothesis

**Raw idea:** build a crypto trading bot for retail investors

## Test Card (Strategyzer)

**We believe** active crypto traders with 1-3 years experience need to automate their trading strategies to execute trades 24/7 without manual intervention because they lack the time and emotional discipline to monitor volatile markets consistently

**To verify this**, we will 5-question customer interview script with 15 target traders to understand their current automation attempts and pain points

**We'll measure** percentage of interviewees who confirm this as their top trading frustration

**We are right if** 12 out of 15 interviewees (80%) rank automated execution as their #1 unmet need within 10 days

## Assumptions (RAT-Ranked)

| Rank | Assumption | Risk | Uncertainty | RAT Score |
|------|-----------|------|-------------|----------|
| 1 | Traders will trust a third-party platform to execute automated trades with their capital | high | high | 9 |
| 2 | Regulatory compliance for automated crypto trading is manageable in target markets | high | medium | 6 |
| 3 | Traders are willing to pay a subscription fee for reliable automation that saves them time | medium | medium | 4 |
| 4 | The target segment has consistent internet access to monitor and adjust automated strategies | low | medium | 2 |
| 5 | Traders want to backtest strategies before automating them with real funds | medium | low | 2 |
| 6 | Active crypto traders have specific strategies they want to automate but lack technical skills to implement them | low | low | 1 |

**Riskiest assumption (rank 1):** Traders will trust a third-party platform to execute automated trades with their capital

## Requirements

- User can create and save custom trading strategies using a visual builder or simple rules
- User can backtest strategies against historical market data with performance metrics
- User can set automated execution with stop-loss and take-profit parameters
- User receives real-time notifications when automated trades are executed

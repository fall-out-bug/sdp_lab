# Discovery Frame

**Raw idea:** replace standups with async AI-generated team summaries

## Problem Statement

Teams waste time in synchronous standup meetings that are often repetitive, poorly attended, and inefficient for capturing and distributing daily progress updates.

## Jobs to Be Done

- Team members need to quickly log daily progress and blockers without interrupting deep work
- Managers need an automated way to synthesize individual updates into a coherent team summary to track progress and identify risks
- Remote/async team members need to stay informed about team progress without being dependent on a specific meeting time

**Appetite:** small

**Scope:** A tool that collects async daily updates from team members, uses AI to generate a concise team summary, and distributes it automatically, replacing the need for a daily synchronous meeting.

# Discovery Experiment Brief

## Format: customer_interview

**Objective:** Validate whether consulting PMs can define useful inter-agent communication protocols at a high level without deep technical expertise.

**Hypothesis:** if we run 5 structured interviews with consulting product managers and ask them to draft a simple agent handoff protocol from a plain-language template, then at least 60% will produce a usable protocol artifact and report high confidence in doing so within 7 days

**Success metric:** 3 of 5 interviewees create a protocol draft that a technical reviewer rates as implementable with minor edits, within 7 days

**Time box:** 7 days

**Testing claim:** Product managers can define communication protocols without deep technical expertise

## Setup Steps

1. Recruit 5 product managers at consulting firms who have designed workflows or delivery playbooks
2. Prepare a 1-page prompt template with 3 common agent handoff scenarios and a simple protocol structure (inputs, outputs, constraints, escalation)
3. Conduct 30-minute interviews, have each PM fill the template live, then score the outputs with a lightweight technical rubric

## Required Tools

- calendar invites
- video call tool
- shared document template
- simple scoring rubric

# Discovery Hypothesis

**Raw idea:** automate product discovery using AI agents

## Test Card (Strategyzer)

**We believe** product managers at Series A startups need to systematically identify and prioritize user problems to build the right solutions because they lack dedicated research teams and cannot afford 6-week discovery cycles

**To verify this**, we will 5-question interview with 10 product managers asking them to walk through their current discovery process and pain points

**We'll measure** percentage of interviewees who identify inconsistent synthesis and prioritization as their top discovery friction

**We are right if** 7 out of 10 interviewees rank inconsistent synthesis and prioritization as their #1 discovery friction within 14 days

## Assumptions (RAT-Ranked)

| Rank | Assumption | Risk | Uncertainty | RAT Score |
|------|-----------|------|-------------|----------|
| 1 | Teams are willing to share qualitative research data in a centralized system | high | high | 9 |
| 2 | Inconsistent synthesis and prioritization is a more painful problem than data collection or prototyping | medium | high | 6 |
| 3 | Product managers will adopt a new tool if it reduces discovery cycle time by 30% | medium | medium | 4 |
| 4 | Existing tools (Notion, Miro, spreadsheets) are insufficient for synthesis and prioritization | medium | low | 2 |
| 5 | Product managers at Series A startups have autonomy over discovery processes | low | low | 1 |

**Riskiest assumption (rank 1):** Teams are willing to share qualitative research data in a centralized system

## Requirements

- User can upload research notes and automatically cluster them into thematic insights
- User can score and rank problem statements based on impact and frequency data
- User can generate prioritized problem briefs with supporting evidence in one click
- User can track validation progress across multiple discovery projects

# Discovery Experiment Brief

## Format: customer_interview

**Objective:** Determine whether infrastructure engineers and platform architects believe a Go-native SDP harness with built-in SDLC gates, RBAC, and multi-LLM orchestration would solve a real pain strongly enough to justify replacing external harnesses.

**Hypothesis:** If we conduct 5 structured interviews with target users and show a concrete Go-native harness concept, then at least 60% will say they would seriously consider adopting it and identify a current workflow pain that it would remove within 7 days.

**Success metric:** 3 out of 5 interviews express strong adoption intent plus a specific pain tied to external harness dependence, within 7 days

**Time box:** 7 days

**Testing claim:** A Go-native SDP harness would provide sufficient SDLC discipline without external tools

## Setup Steps

1. Recruit 5 target participants: 2 infrastructure engineers, 2 platform architects, 1 development team lead who currently uses or evaluates agent-loop/harness tooling.
2. Prepare a 1-page concept prompt with the proposed Go-native SDP harness, built-in gates, RBAC, and multi-LLM orchestration; include 3 simple workflow scenarios to react to.
3. Run 30-minute structured interviews, asking about current harness dependencies, pain severity, willingness to switch, and what would make the native approach mandatory or unacceptable.

## Required Tools

- Calendar scheduling tool
- Video call tool
- Interview script
- One-page concept brief
